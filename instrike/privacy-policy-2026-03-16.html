1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide specific features (like body tracking and leaderboards) and to improve the App's stability.

A. Information You Provide to Us

We utilize Firebase Authentication (Google) to facilitate secure login. While this service manages your credentials, our internal database minimizes personal data storage:

  • User ID: We store a unique identifier linked to your Google account to manage your preferences and game progress.
  • Email & Name: These are processed and stored securely by the Firebase Authentication service to allow you to log in. We do not persist your email or name in our own separate application database, though we may access this information via Firebase for support purposes.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Purchase History: via RevenueCat. We track your subscription status and purchase history to unlock premium features. We do not collect or store your financial information (such as credit card numbers); all transactions are processed securely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • Usage Data: via Google Analytics. We collect anonymous data about how you interact with the App, including your device model, operating system version, and performance data.
  • Crash Reports and Error Monitoring: via Sentry. To ensure the App's stability, we collect diagnostic data when the App crashes or encounters errors. By default, this data may include your IP address, device state information, operating system, device model, and a trace of the actions leading up to the error.

C. Sensitive Permissions: Camera & Body Tracking

Our App requests access to your device’s Camera, but this access is strictly limited:

  • User Control: The camera is only activated when you explicitly select the "Body Tracking" mode within the App. It is never accessed in the background or while using other features.
  • Purpose: The camera input is used solely to detect body movements for gameplay or analysis in real-time.
  • Local Processing: All image data is processed locally on your device. We do not record, save, or transmit video or images of you to our servers or any third party.
  • Revocable: You can revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings. If you do so, the Body Tracking feature will be disabled, but the rest of the App will remain functional.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To create your account and verify your identity (via Google).

  • Billing: To manage your subscriptions and premium entitlements (via RevenueCat).
  • Support: To respond to your inquiries.
  • Improvement & Stability: To analyze usage trends (via Google Analytics) and detect, monitor, and fix technical errors and crashes (via Sentry).
